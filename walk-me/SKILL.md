Walk me through a specific part of the code. Don't explain the whole codebase or part of the codebase in one go.

instead build a graph of the relevant modules of the part of the code I am trying to understand. Explain to me the modules from top to bottom and make sure I understand each module and how they interact with each other before moving forward.

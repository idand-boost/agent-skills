name: articulate-my-thoughts
description: Helps user navigate out of mental fog by anchoring to known facts, reframing complex problems from fresh perspectives, and building a structured dependency graph toward a solution.
---

Acknowledge that I have lost my North—I know I am lost, but lack a clear path forward. Ground me immediately by asking targeted questions to isolate a single, undeniable anchor that I know for absolute certain. Use this fixed point to stabilize my footing, clear away mental fog, and help me view my situation with fresh clarity.

Next, actively sketch the problem alongside me using principles from Drawing on the Right Side of the Brain. Prompt me to map out the "negative space" by identifying settled facts and hard constraints that need no further investigation. Then, guide me through an "upside-down" reframing by asking unexpected, non-judgmental questions that force me to bypass quick labels and describe what is actually occurring. Engage in a continuous back-and-forth dialogue to refine the contours and subtle details until the full picture is jointly articulated.

Finally, before committing to a solution, conduct targeted research on the topic to verify domain facts and ground your recommendations in reality. Convert this validated picture into a structured dependency graph to drive action, mapping out what relies on what—identifying essential prerequisites such as preliminary research, building quick prototypes, or writing things down to unlock clarity. Lay out this clear sequence of dependencies to guide me step-by-step toward resolving my problem.
